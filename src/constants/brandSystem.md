# CareLink.AI Brand Design System

## 1. Brand Personality
- Calm
- Trusted
- Precise
- Human-centered
- Forward-looking
- Premium

## 2. Brand Story
CareLink.AI is a premium digital health experience that brings clarity, speed, and confidence to modern care journeys. It combines human empathy with intelligent infrastructure to make complex healthcare interactions feel effortless and trustworthy.

## 3. Brand Voice
- Confident, reassuring, and elegant
- Write clearly and with calm authority
- Use language that feels human, not clinical
- Prioritize clarity over cleverness
- Sound modern, reassuring, and precise

## 4. Brand Emotion
Security, calm, and confidence.

## 5. Premium Color Palette
- Primary blue: #4d84ff
- Accent teal: #16b6a6
- Success green: #22c55e
- Warning amber: #f59e0b
- Error red: #ef4444
- Neutral slate range from #f8fafc to #0f172a

## 6. Accent Colors
Use the blue and teal family as the primary brand accents. Reserve them for key actions, focus states, and premium emphasis.

## 7. Success
Use green for completed actions, healthy states, and positive confirmation.

## 8. Warning
Use amber for caution, guidance, and pending states.

## 9. Error
Use red for destructive actions, failure states, and urgent alerts.

## 10. Typography Pairing
- Heading: Inter Tight
- Body: Inter
- Code: JetBrains Mono

## 11. Border Radius Rules
- Small: 0.5rem
- Medium: 0.875rem
- Large: 1.125rem
- Extra Large: 1.375rem
- 2XL: 1.75rem
- 3XL: 2rem

## 12. Shadow Rules
- Subtle: soft depth for standard surfaces
- Soft: medium depth for elevated cards
- Premium: strongest depth for major containers and overlays

## 13. Glassmorphism Rules
Use semi-transparent dark surfaces with subtle border and blur. Keep the effect restrained and polished.

## 14. Icon Style
Rounded, soft, slightly outlined, minimal stroke weight.

## 15. Illustration Style
Clean vector forms, calm gradients, subtle depth, and clinical clarity.

## 16. Photography Style
Realistic, warm, human, high trust, natural light, soft contrast.

## 17. Gradient Rules
Use restrained, premium gradients that feel clinical and polished rather than loud.

## 18. Button Philosophy
Quiet confidence, strong hierarchy, minimal friction, calm contrast.

## 19. Card Philosophy
Readable, structured, premium depth, precise spacing, no visual noise.

## 20. Motion Philosophy
Subtle, purposeful, calm, and efficient; never distracting.

## 21. Animation Speed
- Fast: 160ms
- Normal: 220ms
- Slow: 320ms

## 22. Hover Philosophy
Light elevation, subtle brightness, refined feedback, no aggressive motion.

## 23. Empty States
Calm visual clarity, helpful guidance, reassuring copy, clear next step.

## 24. Loading Philosophy
Quiet motion, low contrast, never flashy, always trustworthy.

## 25. Premium UI Principles
Prioritize clarity over decoration. Use restraint to feel elevated. Let spacing and hierarchy do the work.

## 26. Healthcare Trust Principles
Signal reliability through calm consistency. Avoid ambiguity in status and hierarchy. Support fast comprehension in high-pressure moments.

## 27. Accessibility Principles
Maintain readable contrast. Support keyboard and screen-reader flows. Ensure touch targets remain generous and clear.

## 28. Design Tokens
- Background: #050816
- Surface: rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.68)
- Text: #f8fafc
- Muted text: #cbd5e1
- Border: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)
- Brand: #4d84ff
- Accent: #16b6a6

## 29. Component Naming Rules
Use descriptive, product-oriented names for components and semantic names for tokens.

## 30. Page Spacing Rules
- Mobile padding: 1rem
- Tablet padding: 1.5rem
- Desktop padding: 2rem
- Wide padding: 3rem
